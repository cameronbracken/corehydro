   admit C++ versus C# differences of `4.5e-8`, `6.4e-8`, and `4.4e-9`. This directly contradicts
   the completion criterion in
   `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-22-numerics-v2.2.0-upstream-sync.md:617`, the claim in
   `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-22-numerics-v2.2.0-public-surface-audit.md:106`, and the claim in
   `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-22-numerics-v2.2.0-ledger.md:531`.

   The uncommitted attempt to restore `1e-9` without changing the assertions is not a fix. A
   focused run of `core/build/test_fixtures` against only `nuts.json` failed 3 of 78 checks:

   - MAP parameter 0: expected `15152.579886079518`, got `15152.579207469083`;
   - MAP parameter 1: expected `5677.191270920194`, got `5677.1916336105514`;
   - MAP fitness: expected `-479.81146279116`, got `-479.81146068872556`.

   Diagnose and remove the arithmetic divergence, or follow the repository's established chaotic
   sampler policy: remove the unstable cross-runtime trajectory assertions, preserve deterministic
   and same-core coverage in direct tests, and document exactly what is no longer claimed. Do not
   keep either the widened tolerances or the failing `1e-9` edit. Re-run the focused C++ fixture,
   all three package fixture runners, and the C# oracle after the correction.
